WelNex Website – Major Product Update
Hi,
We've pivoted WelNex from a Commercial Real Estate education platform into India's
AI-powered Alternative Investment Marketplace.
This is no longer just an educational website. The website should be built as a scalable
fintech marketplace where investors can discover, compare, understand, research, and
eventually access alternative investment opportunities through trusted investment
partners.
Please read the entire document before starting, as there are significant architecture changes.

Overall Vision
WelNex should combine the best aspects of:





Skyscanner (Discovery Marketplace)
Zerodha Varsity (Education)

The website should not feel like a blog.
It should feel like a premium fintech marketplace.
